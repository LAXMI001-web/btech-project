How FA3-CLIP works

Spatial + frequency fusion

FA3-CLIP pairs a CLIP vision transformer with a frequency-domain branch built on the Discrete Cosine Transform. Generation artifacts that are nearly invisible in raw pixels — blending seams, upsampling checkerboarding, GAN/diffusion texture — leave a distinct signature in frequency space, so fusing both views catches attacks that a spatial-only model misses.

Attack-agnostic prompts

Instead of a separate detector per attack type (printed photo, replay, faceswap, reenactment, diffusion-generated), FA3-CLIP learns generic "live" and "fake" prompts in CLIP's language branch, so one unified model covers physical and digital attacks alike.

What the spectrum strip shows

The bar strip at the top of the page is a live row-wise high-frequency energy profile computed from the most recently analyzed image — the same kind of signal the frequency branch consumes.
